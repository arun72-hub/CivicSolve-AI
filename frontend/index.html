FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How does CivicSolve AI identify the correct government authority?
Our NLP and spatial classification engine cross-references the problem category, description keywords, and GPS coordinates against administrative boundaries and departmental mandates — e.g. Roads → PWD, Water → BWSSB, Power → BESCOM.
What happens if an authority misses the SLA deadline?
CivicSolve AI triggers a 4-tier escalation protocol: automated reminders (Day 0–2), Zonal Senior Officer (Tier 2), Department Head (Tier 3), and District Magistrate & Municipal Commissioner review docket (Tier 4).
How do universities collaborate on civic problems?
Chronic civic issues are published to the Academic Innovation Hub. Faculty mentors and student teams apply via AI Smart Matching to engineer prototypes. Approved prototypes enter field pilot testing with municipal clearance and CSR sponsorship.
How are duplicate complaints prevented?
Before submission, the AI performs a real-time semantic and geographic proximity scan within a 1.5 km radius. If a matching report exists, the citizen is invited to upvote or add evidence to the existing ticket instead of creating a duplicate.
Is the platform fully mobile responsive?
Yes — 100% responsive across smartphones, tablets, laptops, and desktops. Features include mobile slide drawers, bottom navigation, direct GPS integration, and browser WebRTC camera access for on-site reporting.
